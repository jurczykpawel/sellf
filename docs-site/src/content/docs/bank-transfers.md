---
title: Traditional bank transfers
description: Configure payment terms, access and invoices for offline orders.
---

Enable **Traditional bank transfer** under **Settings → Payments**. Enter the account holder and a valid Polish IBAN or NRB (checksum verified), optionally the bank name, and a title template containing `{order_number}`. The preview shows the resulting title.Long references wrap in the preview, including on mobile, without truncating the value. Choose a deadline of 1–60 days (default 14), whether to grant access on placement (default on), and the unpaid order limit per email (default 3).

In each product's checkout profile, enable the buyer module and choose eligible buyer types: person, organization, or both. No profile or an empty selection means no transfer. Subscription, bundle and custom-price products do not support this checkout profile. Changing buyer type updates the offered payment methods.

Configure `ALTCHA_HMAC_KEY` on the server: transfer orders always require ALTCHA, including shops that use another captcha for login.

Buyers choose **Pay online** or **Traditional bank transfer**, accept the terms and complete ALTCHA. Transfer orders do not use Stripe. The **Order with obligation to pay** button opens the order's signed instructions link, valid for 61 days. It includes the account, amount, title, deadline and **Print / save PDF**. Keep this private link; it needs no login.

When enabled, access starts on placement; event seats remain reserved until payment or cancellation. Licenses, affiliate commissions and `purchase.completed` wait for payment; the buyer then gets the license key by email. The deadline in the mails and on the order page is the same: the reservation end while it runs, otherwise the payment term. In **Payments**, filter **Awaiting bank transfer**, check the deadline and highlighted overdue rows. The dashboard shows the overdue count. **Mark as paid** requires the exact full booked amount and the date received (UTC), between the order day and today (API v1 answers `paid_at_before_order` / `paid_at_future` otherwise). **Cancel order** requires a reason, returns seats and coupon use, and removes access granted by that order while preserving other entitlements.

Sellf has no separate transactional email service. Transfer instructions, reminders and cancellation notices use signed webhooks by default. With buyer emails enabled, Sellf also sends them through Supabase Auth with the order link; see [Buyer emails](/buyer-emails/). A sign-in link needed to claim access granted on placement is still sent when buyer notification emails are off. When self-hosting, update both Auth templates (`magic-link.html`, `confirmation.html`) and their subjects (`mail_kind`) in the Auth configuration. Sellers can send their own messages through `order.placed` webhooks (for example n8n or Listmonk). `order.cancelled` and `order.overdue` support follow-up automation; payment uses `purchase.completed`. API automation can use `POST /api/v1/payments/:id/mark-paid` and `/cancel` with `payments:write`.

With invoicing in **auto** mode, placement schedules an unpaid invoice with the order's deadline. Invoice failures do not block the order and retry through `invoices-retry`; payment updates the existing invoice rather than creating another. After cancellation, the invoice section links to the provider for a manual correction; mark it corrected once completed.

Schedule the authenticated `/api/cron` jobs: `bank-transfer-overdue` daily, `fulfillment-resume` and `invoices-retry` every minute, `transfer-reservations` every 5 minutes, using `CRON_SECRET` as the bearer credential. Orders without a timed reservation are never cancelled automatically.

The first transfer-instructions email follows the access policy saved on the order. With access on placement, a new buyer is asked to confirm the email to open access without waiting for payment; a buyer with a confirmed account is told that access is already active. Both messages remind the buyer to pay by the deadline. With access after payment, it explains that access waits for the booked transfer. Later shop configuration changes do not change that order’s policy.

**Timed reservation (events).** In the transfer settings configure: timed reservation (on by default), reminder after N hours (default 24), release after N hours (default 48; must be later than the reminder) and the extension after "I have sent the transfer" (default 72 h). The payment term (invoice deadline) stays the configured number of days; the reservation is separate. After the reminder the buyer gets a mail with the order link; on that page they can click **I have sent the transfer** once (with a short note), which extends the hold and shows a badge in the payment details so you check the statement. After the reservation deadline (before the automatic cancellation runs) the button stays, but clicking it only tells you: the badge in the payment details is marked **after the reservation deadline**, the reservation is not extended and the order is cancelled on time. The buyer sees "We have let the seller know. If the transfer arrives after the cancellation, the seller can accept it." (see **Transfer after cancellation** below). After the deadline the order is cancelled automatically: the seat, tier price and coupon use return to the pool, access from that order is withdrawn and the buyer gets a mail. A transfer is not offered when the session starts before the end of the reservation window. Regular products never expire.

**Invoice = no expiry.** When an invoice is issued for an unpaid order (in Sellf, or an integration reports it with `POST /api/v1/payments/:id/invoice-issued`; in the panel: **Invoice issued outside Sellf**), the order is "invoice issued, awaiting payment": the seat is held without expiry, even past the due date. Only **Cancel order** releases it; then issue a correction. In invoicing **auto** mode Sellf issues the invoice at placement, so such orders do not expire.

**A cancelled order cannot be paid by the buyer.** This holds for both reasons: cancelled by the administrator or released after the reservation deadline. The order page shows "Order cancelled" with a link to the product (the buyer who still wants it places a new order), without the **Pay online** and **I have sent the transfer** buttons, and the server refuses payment as well (`order_cancelled`). If the buyer was on the Stripe payment page at the moment of cancellation, Sellf expires that page; a payment that still gets through is refunded automatically, and the order stays cancelled.

**Transfer after cancellation.** The system never revives a cancelled order on its own; the administrator decides. In the cancelled order's details Sellf shows a preview: the order price and today's price, the seats left in the session and in the order's price tier (or that acceptance goes over the limit) and the coupon state. The administrator then chooses **Accept late transfer at order price** (full amount, receipt date, reason) or **Do not accept**, which changes nothing: the order stays cancelled and the money is returned outside Sellf. Acceptance grants access again, re-uses the coupon even over its limit, records `order.reopened` in the audit log with the reason, and sends the `order.reopened` and `purchase.completed` webhooks. An order whose invoice was corrected cannot be reopened.

**Accepting over the limit.** When the session or the order's price tier has no seat left, acceptance is a second, explicit decision: the panel enables the button only after **Accept over the limit** is ticked, and the server refuses it otherwise (`oversold_confirmation_required`). The order is then marked oversold and the audit entry records `oversold_confirmed`.

**Via the API.** `GET /api/v1/payments/:id/late-transfer-preview` (`payments:read`) returns the same preview for a cancelled transfer order: `unitPrice` and `unitPriceToday` (major units), `amount` (minor units), `seats` (`remaining` in the session, `tierRemaining` in the order's price tier, `null` = unlimited, `quantity`, `oversold`, `started`), `coupon` and `invoiceCorrected`; 404 for any other order. Accept with `POST /api/v1/payments/:id/mark-paid` (`payments:write`) and `{"amount", "paidAt", "acceptCancelled": true, "reason"}`; when `seats.oversold` is true also send `"acceptOversold": true`, otherwise the answer is **409** `CONFLICT` with `error.details.code: ["oversold_confirmation_required"]` (other refusals, e.g. a wrong amount, stay 400 `VALIDATION_ERROR`).

Every state change sends a signed webhook: `order.placed`, `order.payment_reminder`, `order.transfer_declared`, `order.invoice_issued`, `order.cancelled`, `order.reopened`, `purchase.completed` (see [Webhooks](/webhooks/)).

**Pay online from the link.** The order page (linked from the mails and from `payUrl` in webhooks) has a **Pay online** button. An unpaid order is paid at its saved amount (price and seat stay reserved) through Stripe Checkout. A cancelled order cannot be paid online. After the reservation deadline no new online payment can be started (the server answers `payment_window_closed`), and until the automatic release runs the order page says **The payment deadline has passed — the order will be cancelled soon** instead of the transfer details; a payment started before the deadline may finish, and the automatic release waits for it at most 30 minutes after the deadline, then cancels the order (a payment that still lands is refunded). An order paid online stays a transfer order but carries a Stripe payment, so refunds work as for card orders. If an administrator marks the transfer paid or cancels the order while an online payment is in progress, Sellf expires the Stripe page; a payment that still gets through is refunded automatically.

## Open receipts and printing

An open unpaid receipt refreshes at the reservation deadline, every 15 seconds and when the window regains focus. It removes payment instructions when the deadline passes or the seller cancels the order. **My purchases** labels cancelled transfers **Cancelled — do not pay this order** and unpaid transfers **Awaiting bank transfer**.

**Print / save PDF** omits cookie-preference controls and their footer, so a short receipt fits on one A4 page without an extra interface-only page.

Each validated order link has its own refresh allowance, so buyers of different orders do not share one refresh budget.

Cancellation removes the access period granted by that transfer, including when another purchase later extended the same product. Other paid periods, free grants and administrator access remain. Refunding a paid transfer follows the same rule; repeated notifications preserve later manual changes. Cancelling a transfer that did not grant access on placement leaves pre-existing access unchanged. A placement attempt that leaves existing access unchanged records no grant period. Cancelling it leaves that access untouched; paying it later starts its paid period when payment is received. Payment grants each missing buyer/product period, including bundle components, while keeping periods already granted on placement unchanged.
